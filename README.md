# 🪔 செவிப் பொன் சேர்ப்பு விழா | Premium Tamil Ear Piercing Ceremony Digital Invitation

A royal, cinematic, interactive single-page digital invitation website for the traditional Tamil **Ear Piercing Ceremony (செவிப் பொன் சேர்ப்பு விழா)** of **R. கிருஷ்வந்த் & R. சுபத்ரா (R. Krishwanth & R. Subhadra)**.

This website combines:
- **Reference 1 (`https://vignesh-keerthana.pages.dev`)**: Complete UI & UX, royal ivory-maroon-gold color scheme, temple arch framing, smooth parallax, Polaroid scroll tilt animation, and audio equalizer.
- **Reference 2 (`https://earpiercing.vercel.app/`)**: 100% factual source of truth for ceremony information, Tamil terminology, family names, timings, venue in Madurai, traditions, and WhatsApp RSVP.

## 🌟 Visual & Interactive Features (Combining References 1 & 2)

1. **3D Carved Wooden Palace Doors (Image 1 Ref)**:
   - Double carved doors with gold monogram (**கி & சு**), brass studs, and handles.
   - Button: `✦ OPEN THE INVITATION ✦` (or `✦ அழைப்பிதழைத் திறக்கவும் ✦`).
   - Double doors swing open with 3D perspective, rose confetti, and divine music!

2. **Temple Arched Hero Section (Image 2 Ref)**:
   - Ivory `#FAF6EE` background, traditional monogram `கி&சு`, language toggle `தமிழ் / English`, and menu button.
   - Architectural temple arch with gold pillars, hanging jasmine garlands framing the children's portrait.
   - `SAVE THE DATE` with gold diamond filigree divider.
   - Large royal serif title: **R. KRISHWANTH & R. SUBHADRA**.
   - Live Muhurtham countdown timer.

3. **Velvet Maroon Ceremony Timeline (Image 3 Ref)**:
   - Deep rich maroon velvet `#420f17` background.
   - "Ceremony Celebration" with large gold date numeral **29 NOVEMBER SUNDAY**.
   - **ADD TO CALENDAR** button.
   - Vertical gold timeline line with diamond node markers and translucent cards for Ganapathi Pooja, Muhurtham, and Banana Leaf Feast.

4. **Scroll-Animated Polaroid Story Gallery (Image 4 Ref)**:
   - Deep maroon background.
   - "Little Moments" / "மழலை நினைவுகள்" in gold script.
   - Realistic Polaroid photo cards with washi tape stickers.
   - **GSAP ScrollTrigger**: As the user scrolls, each Polaroid glides up and tilts with natural physics.

5. **Auspicious Muhurtham & Venue**:
   - V.R. Mahal, Thirunagar, Madurai with photo, landmark, Google Maps interactive embed, and 1-tap "Get Directions" navigation.

6. **Blessings & WhatsApp RSVP**:
   - Interactive guest wishes card with celebratory confetti.
   - Guest count selector and direct WhatsApp RSVP to `+91 98401 23456`.

7. **Thank You Jharokha Sunset Page (Image 5 Ref)**:
   - Palace jharokha arch overlooking a golden sunset with glowing candles.
   - "Thank You" gold script + "UNTIL WE MEET AGAIN, KEEP US IN YOUR PRAYERS" + Family sign-off.

8. **Floating Equalizer Music Button**:
   - The opening buttons start the looping `/music.mpeg` track; the floating circular control shows animated gold bars while it plays.
